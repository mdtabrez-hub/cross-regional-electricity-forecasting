# Claims and Evidence

Every quantitative statement in the manuscript is rendered from `results/manuscript_numbers.json`
(generated by `src/manuscript_numbers.py` from the files below). This table maps the main
claims to their evidence. Status: **S** supported, **P** partially supported / caveated.
