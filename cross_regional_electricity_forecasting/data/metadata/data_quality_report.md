## Known limitations

* No weather covariates are included in EIA-930.
* BA boundaries and membership change over time (e.g. utilities joining SWPP/MISO, Western Energy Imbalance Market participation does not change BA demand but BA consolidations do). Level shifts are part of the temporal distribution shift studied.
* EIA-930 values are operational data subject to revision; PUDL screening (Ruggles et al., 2020 method) flags anomalies but cannot guarantee correctness.
* BA day-ahead forecasts have unknown issuance times and use weather forecasts; they are a reference, not a like-for-like benchmark.
* BAs are spatially correlated; observations across regions are not independent.
